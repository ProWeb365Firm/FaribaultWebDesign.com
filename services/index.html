Frequently Asked Questions

1. Do I have to purchase every service (web design, SEO, ads) together, or can I start with one?

Generally, businesses can begin with a single service, like a website rebuild or local SEO, and add others later as budget and priorities allow, rather than committing to a full package upfront. The advantage of consolidating vendors over time is coordination, not an all-or-nothing purchase requirement.

2. How is pricing structured across web design, SEO, and ongoing marketing services?

Pricing models differ by service type. Web design is often a one-time or phased project cost, while SEO, ads, and content marketing are billed as ongoing monthly retainers tied to continued management and reporting. A written quote following the discovery and audit phase should clarify which costs are one-time versus recurring for your specific mix of services.

3. How quickly should I expect to see results after launching a new website or starting local SEO?

Website launches illustrate immediate changes in look and functionality, but SEO and AI search visibility improvements typically take three to six months to materialize as search engines and AI tools re-crawl and re-evaluate a site’s authority signals. Paid advertising (Google and Meta Ads) tends to produce the fastest measurable results, often within the first few weeks of a campaign.

4. What if I already have a website I like and only want digital marketing services?

Local SEO, content marketing, and advertising campaigns can usually run on an existing website without requiring a full rebuild, provided the site has no major technical issues limiting SEO performance. An initial audit typically identifies whether the current site needs fixes before marketing investment will produce full results.

5. How do you measure whether AI search optimization is actually working?

Tracking AI search visibility involves monitoring whether a business is cited in tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google AI Overviews for relevant local queries, requiring different tracking methods than traditional Google Analytics or rank-tracking tools. Monthly reporting for AI search efforts should include specific evidence of citation appearances paired with traditional traffic and ranking metrics.

6. What happens if I'm unhappy with the direction of my website design during the build phase?

The build-and-launch phase includes client review at each milestone, meaning a business has structured checkpoints to request changes before the site goes live rather than only seeing a finished product. Raising concerns early, during the strategy and scope phase, also reduces the likelihood of major revisions being needed later in the build.

7. Can Faribault Web Design work with businesses outside the specific southern Minnesota towns listed?

While the agency’s stated service area centers on Faribault, Northfield, Rice County, and nearby southern Minnesota communities, web design and digital marketing services can often be delivered remotely for businesses located outside that immediate footprint. Confirm directly whether in-person meetings are available or whether service would be handled entirely through calls and video consultations for a given location.
