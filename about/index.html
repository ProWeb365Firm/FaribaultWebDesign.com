Common Questions About Working With Us

What to expect when you start a project with Faribault Web Design.

1. How do we know if Faribault Web Design is the right fit for our business?

The best fit usually comes down to expectations and communication style. Faribault Web Design works best with organizations valuing clear conversations, realistic planning, and collaboration throughout the process. If you’re looking for a partner who takes time to understand your goals, explains options honestly, and focuses on long-term value rather than quick wins, there’s a strong chance we’ll work well together. We’re always upfront about whether a project makes sense for both sides, even if that means steering you in a different direction.

2. What does the process look like before a project officially begins?

Before any design or development work starts, our team spends time learning about your business/organization, audience, and challenges. This includes discussions around your goals, existing systems, timelines, and budget considerations. That discovery phase allows us to recommend solutions that are practical and aligned with your priorities. Nothing moves forward until expectations are clear, questions are answered, and both sides feel confident about the plan.

3. Can you work with an existing website, or do you only build new ones?

Yes, many of our clients come to us with existing websites. In some cases, a redesign or rebuild is the best option; in others, targeted improvements or structural updates can extend the life of what already exists. We take the time to evaluate your current site’s performance, usability, and flexibility before making recommendations. The goal is to go with the most effective and responsible path forward, not automatically start from scratch.

4. How involved do we need to be during the project?

Your level of involvement can vary, but collaboration is always part of the process. We provide guidance through decisions, provide clear feedback points, and handle the technical details, while relying on your insight into your business and customers. Some clients prefer a more hands-on role, while others want us to manage most of the process; both approaches can work. What matters most is open communication and timely input when it’s needed.

5. What happens if our needs change after the project starts?

It’s common for ideas to evolve once a project is underway. When changes arise, we talk through the impact on scope, timeline, and budget before moving forward. This ensures there are no surprises and that decisions are made intentionally. The end result is to remain flexible without compromising quality or clarity, and we approach adjustments with the same transparency as the original plan.

6. How do you approach pricing and project estimates?

We believe pricing should be clear, fair, and tied to the actual work involved. Every project is scoped based on your specific needs rather than pre-set packages. Before any work begins, you’ll receive a detailed outline of what’s included, along with clear expectations surrounding costs and timelines. If something isn’t necessary, or won’t add real value, we’ll say so. That honesty helps clients make informed decisions and build trust from the start.

7. What kind of support can we expect after the project is completed?

After launch, we stay available to support your website and digital strategy as your business grows. This includes maintenance, updates, troubleshooting, and guidance as new opportunities or challenges arise. Some clients prefer ongoing support plans, while others reach out as needed. Either way, the relationship doesn’t end at launch, we’re here to help you continue moving forward with confidence.
