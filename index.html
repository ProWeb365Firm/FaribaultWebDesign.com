    FAQs About Website Design and Marketing

    Everything you need to know about working with Faribault Web Design — answered honestly.

    1. Do you build new websites or redesign existing ones?

    We do both. Some businesses need a completely new site, while others benefit from overhauling what they already have. During our consultation, we evaluate your current website’s performance, structure, and goals to recommend the most effective path forward.

    2. Do you use WordPress for website development?

    Yes. WordPress is our preferred platform since it’s flexible, scalable, and widely supported. It allows for straightforward content management, reliable SEO foundations, and long-term control. For eCommerce websites, we use WooCommerce to create secure and customizable online stores.

    3. Will my website be mobile-friendly and responsive?

    Absolutely. Every website designed is fully responsive, meaning it works seamlessly across mobile phones, tablets, and desktop devices. Mobile optimization is essential for user experience, search rankings, and AI-driven search visibility.

    4. How long does a typical website project take?

    Most projects take between 4 and 8 weeks, depending on complexity, scope, and content readiness. We provide clear timelines and milestones to effectively manage expectations throughout the process.

    5. Do you help with On-page SEO for AI search optimization?

    Yes. On-page SEO is built into our web design process from the start. We optimize site structure, content clarity, performance, and technical elements so search engines and AI-powered tools can easily understand and recommend your business.

    6. What is AI search optimization, and why is it important?

    AI search optimization focuses on helping AI-driven platforms accurately summarize and surface your business. This includes clear messaging, helpful content, standout performance, and trust signals. As AI plays a larger role in search and recommendations, this optimization is increasingly critical.

    7. Will I be able to update my website myself?

    Yes. We build websites that are seamless to manage without technical expertise. You’ll be able to update text, images, and basic content on your own. We also provide guidance so you feel confident making updates.

    8. Do you offer ongoing website support and maintenance?

    Yes. We offer ongoing support and maintenance services as additional services for security, updates, and performance. This includes software updates, performance monitoring, and assistance with improvements or changes as needed.

    9. How involved will I be in the website process?

    You’ll be involved at key stages, such as strategy discussions and design feedback, while we handle the technical work. Our goal is to keep the process collaborative but efficient and stress-free.

    10. Is there a free consultation available?

    Yes. We offer a free, no-obligation consultation to discuss your website, goals, and challenges. This helps determine the best solution for your business and whether we’re a good fit.
